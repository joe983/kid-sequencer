# UK Garage producer signatures — v2, CREW ERA (R34)

The six garage strains, re-anchored at the owner's direction to the 2000–2003
UK Garage **crew era** — *"So Solid Crew, Pay As You Go Cartel, More Fire
Crew, Heartless Crew"* + Artful Dodger (owner-added) + MJ Cole (kept from v1;
era-legit). The 2023–25 revival anchors (Sammy Virji / Interplanetary
Criminal / Conducta / Silva Bumpa / salute) are RETIRED for garage.

The era's defining property is the **MPC 16th shuffle at 61–66%** (engine
swing 0.22–0.32; the old 0.16 ≈ 58% sat below the band — the root of "no
bounce, no swing"), applied ARRANGEMENT-WIDE: bass and stab onsets ride the
same swing clock as the drums (`swung_beat`, R34). Mixes are DRY and punchy —
minimal reverb (the "too washy" complaint is era-inauthentic as well as
unpleasant). Kid-safe mappings: gunshot→knock-stab, air-horn→party horn,
siren→zap riser, MC chat→crowd-"hey"/whistle; rewind/spinback + dub siren are
era-core and already in the engine. Sources: Attack Magazine (UKG / Rolling
2-Step / Garage Shuffle dissections), MusicRadar, Sound On Sound (Mark Hill),
RBMA (Wookie), Producer School, Studio Brootle; full citations in the R34
research output.

Format per entry: BPM · SWING (engine units) · GRID (DRUM_SKELETONS["garage"]
index) · DRUMS · BASS · CHORDS/PADS · LEAD/HOOK · FX · MOOD · HOOK-ID.

---

### So Solid Crew  (`crewdark`) — "21 Seconds", "Oh No (Sentimental Things)"
- **BPM:** 138–140 · target **140** · **SWING 0.24** · **GRID 2** (sparse A)
- **DRUMS:** The sparsest 2-step — two kick anchors (1 + the "and of 3"), the missing beats ARE the identity; prominent rim ghosts; bar-2 downbeat kick drop.
- **BASS:** Warm minor 8-bar sub, long anchors + one swung pickup; never on 2/4.
- **CHORDS/PADS:** Dark minor; staccato **orchestral string stabs** + occasional held cinematic line (VSCO strings / strings_pad).
- **LEAD/HOOK:** The string stab riff + a dark short vocal chop.
- **FX:** String swell (reverse-reverb), knock-stab, spinback.
- **MOOD:** Cinematic, moody, headline-dark (kid-safe: dramatic, never menacing).
- **HOOK-ID:** Staccato string stab on a swung sparse 2-step with beat 4 SILENT.

### Heartless Crew  (`partybounce`)
- **BPM:** 136–140 · target **138** · **SWING 0.30 (max bounce)** · **GRID 4** (4x4-shuffle C)
- **DRUMS:** Four-floor + bar-end ghost kick, heavily shuffled tops — tambourine/shaker contour + conga flavour; NOT techno: the swing makes it garage.
- **BASS:** Bouncing octave sub ON each kick (octave-pop feel), short and springy.
- **CHORDS/PADS:** Bright major/dominant **organ/piano skank** on every offbeat 8th.
- **LEAD/HOOK:** Organ skank + crowd-chant chop ("hey!").
- **FX:** Crowd "hey!", party horn, whistle — the carnival kit.
- **MOOD:** Carnival, grinning, party MC energy (instrumental).
- **HOOK-ID:** Organ skank on every offbeat over a shuffled four-floor + chant chop.

### More Fire Crew  (`stabriddim`) — "Oi!" (Platinum 45)
- **BPM:** 134–136 · target **135** · **SWING 0.22** (rowdier = slightly straighter) · **GRID 3** (boink B, harder)
- **DRUMS:** Grid B with harder kicks and the bar-end double-kick; skip-hat pairs; proto-grime snap.
- **BASS:** None separate — **the square stab IS the bass** (Music-2000 palette, bass_fm knock).
- **CHORDS/PADS:** Single riff, minimal/no pad harmony (pads sit out over half the takes).
- **LEAD/HOOK:** The **square-wave 8-bar stab riff**, rude and cheeky.
- **FX:** Dub-delay throw, knock-stab.
- **MOOD:** Rowdy, cheeky, proto-grime playground energy.
- **HOOK-ID:** A square stab riff looped 8 bars functioning as bass AND hook.

### Pay As U Go Cartel  (`coldbass`) — "Champagne Dance" (Geeneus/DJ Target)
- **BPM:** 136–140 · target **138** · **SWING 0.24** · **GRID 2** (sparse A, icy thin hats)
- **DRUMS:** Sparse 2-step, thin cold hat lanes, minimal seasoning.
- **BASS:** **The 8-bar sub riff IS the hook** — dark, minimal, rolling; lead stack sits out ~35% of takes.
- **CHORDS/PADS:** Sparse cold synth-stab answers only (pluck/FM tines).
- **LEAD/HOOK:** The bassline, answered by a cold stab.
- **FX:** Zap riser, spinback.
- **MOOD:** Icy, minimal, night-bus (kid-safe: mysterious, not menacing).
- **HOOK-ID:** A dark sub riff with a lone cold stab answer on a swung sparse 2-step.

### MJ Cole  (`sincere`) — "Sincere" (kept from v1)
- **BPM:** 128–132 · target **130** · **SWING 0.26** · **GRID 2** (precise, softest velocities)
- **DRUMS:** Immaculate 2-step, brushed/tight rims, silky hats, understated.
- **BASS:** Soft round walking bass, musical, jazzy.
- **CHORDS/PADS:** **Rhodes/grand piano** min7/9 voicings — real-musician harmony.
- **LEAD/HOOK:** The Rhodes/piano figure + a soulful sung chop.
- **FX:** Soft string swell only; minimal candy.
- **MOOD:** Elegant, sincere, musicianly.
- **HOOK-ID:** A min9 Rhodes call-and-answer over a soft swung 2-step.

### Artful Dodger  (`boinkpop`) — "Re-Rewind", "Moving Too Fast"
- **BPM:** 130–134 · target **132** · **SWING 0.28** · **GRID 3** (the canonical "boink")
- **DRUMS:** Grid B — kick 1+7 with the swung ghost pickup; crisp bright top; hand-played looseness.
- **BASS:** **GM organ bass** (the JV1080 "Moving Too Fast" sound — bass_organ) popping on the kick anchors.
- **CHORDS/PADS:** Major-key R&B progression, e-piano/warm.
- **LEAD/HOOK:** **Music-box/kalimba toy-GM lead** (the era's actual hit hooks were GM toy patches — perfect for a kids app) + a pitched sung vocal chop.
- **FX:** Spinback (the "Re-Rewind" signature), reverse cymbal.
- **MOOD:** Sunny, playful, chart-pop.
- **HOOK-ID:** A music-box motif + vocal-chop pickup over the boink 2-step.
