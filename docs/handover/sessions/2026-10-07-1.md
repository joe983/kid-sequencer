## Decisions & Rationale

- Removed the portrait print fallback: Chromium evaluates the print `orientation` query against the printer's default paper, so it fired on landscape pages and clipped every sheet. `@page A4 landscape` alone is reliable.
- Corner marks sized off the full view's 64px cell on every sheet, but the mark-to-content GAP keeps each view's own cell: a 64-cell gap on /03 crowded its bottom row and the scanner merged two 8ths (a scan-test caught it).
- Score is /03-only and read-only: owner judged busier views' scores too complicated, and removed drag-to-change-pitch. The print "Empty sheet / Score" chooser was built then replaced by "print what's on screen".
- /03 one-note-per-beat on tap = REPLACE the other row's note (not refuse); undoable.
- 3/4 is a per-page-load URL param (`cols` is a const), switching is a page load with a sessionStorage handoff (`TS_HANDOFF_KEY`); notes past the shorter bar are clipped to the longest legal length, beat-4 notes dropped. Rejected: making `cols` mutable at runtime.
- In 3/4: AI hidden (engine hard-codes 4/4), Print/Scan hidden (no template), other drum styles greyed (owner: "only a default drum pattern").
- Scan-to-beat on /02 snaps notes ≥ a beat to the beat start, 8ths to the beat/half, leaves 16ths alone — mirrors what tapping can place.
