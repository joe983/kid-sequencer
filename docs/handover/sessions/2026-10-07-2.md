## Decisions & Rationale

- **The first fix was not the best one, and was replaced after the owner asked me to double-check.** It still allowed a rebuild every 10 s, waited only 900 ms for the clock, and merely reordered tick() (audio before DOM) — which does not remove lateness, because notes were timed from tick time. Replaced by one context + slot scheduling. Rejected: just raising `AUDIO_AHEAD_S` (shifts every note equally, absorbs no jitter).
- **Kept a bounded rebuild path (`MAX_AUDIO_REBUILDS` 2) rather than deleting rebuilds:** the >25 s idle reset dates from the original Jan 2026 upload and was probably a workaround for an earlier dead-after-idle symptom, so a genuinely frozen "running" context still gets a last-resort rebuild.
- **`SCHED_LEAD_S` = 100 ms** (the web.dev "tale of two clocks" figure). Drift correction between the timer and the audio clock is 1 %/step capped at 1 ms: a 5 %/2 ms version measured 3 ms of added jitter because `currentTime` readings are noisy.
- **Grid restart threshold (2 steps) must stay below the timer's freeze guard (4 steps).** A draft with both at "4" could let the timer jump ahead of a grid that then dropped every following step — silence at 200 bpm after a stall.
- **Visuals are deferred to the sound's slot** with `setTimeout`; `stop()` cancels them (`_visTimers`), `_lessonSoftStop` deliberately doesn't (the last step's visuals should finish).
- **Two commits, handover pointer left out.** Split with a throwaway hunk-picker script (not in the repo; `git add -p` isn't available here). The 9-line "Session Handover" block in CLAUDE.md was kept out of the audio commit because it links to `docs/handover/`, which is untracked.
- **Pushed straight to `main`, no preview channel**, on the owner's "just commit and push" after being told it was Chrome-only and the /02 scan change shared the file.
- Voices outside a tick (lesson demos, previews) keep the old `currentTime + 10 ms` placement (`_stepAudioAt === null`).
